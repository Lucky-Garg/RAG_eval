Acme Tech Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days of incurring the expense. Claims submitted later than 30 days will be rejected.

Daily meal allowance during business travel is 1,500 INR for domestic travel and 40 USD for international travel.

Flights must be booked in economy class for journeys under 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and requires VP approval.

Original receipts are required for any expense above 500 INR. Claims are reimbursed within 10 working days of manager approval.

A work-from-home internet allowance of 1,000 INR per month is paid to all employees on the hybrid schedule.
