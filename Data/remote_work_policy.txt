Acme Tech Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The remaining 2 days must be spent in the office, and Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory office days.

New joiners must work from the office for their first 30 days for onboarding. After this, they become eligible for the hybrid schedule.

Employees working remotely must be available on Slack between 10 AM and 4 PM local time. A company VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems.

Employees can request a fully remote arrangement for up to 60 days per year for medical or family reasons. This needs approval from both the manager and HR.
