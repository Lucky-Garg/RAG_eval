Acme Tech Leave Policy

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Any leave beyond 8 days expires on 31 March.

Sick leave: Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks fully paid. Paternity leave is 2 weeks fully paid and must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for emergencies and sick leave.
